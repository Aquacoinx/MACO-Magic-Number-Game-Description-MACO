PRIZE PER COMPLETED POT

How to Play

  1. Make a new $MACO purchase worth at least $10.
  2. Hold at least $10 worth of $MACO when eligibility is checked.
  3. Choose one number between 1 and 10.
  4. Submit your wallet and qualifying transaction.
  5. Every 11 verified entries form one pot.
  6. A winning number is randomly generated.
  7. If multiple players match, one matching player is randomly selected.
  8. If nobody matches, the pot follows the published rollover rules.
Existing holdings alone do not qualify. Only verified new purchases count. Each qualifying transaction can be used once.
